# Discovery method and audit boundary

[OBSERVED] This is an isolated research branch of FetianOS. Discovery began from
constraints on information, choices, equivalence, physical erasure, and
observation, not from an existing project architecture. Only this research tree
is changed. No runtime integration is authorized by an unverified hypothesis.

[HYPOTHESIS] The opportunity is a capability gap under *equal assumptions*, not
a new name or a speedup against an intentionally weak baseline. The catalogue
contains 100 authored statements in 20 families and all six requested discovery
methods. These are candidate claims, including intentionally strong falsifiable
extensions, not 100 novel ideas. Family members share assumptions and a
mathematical model; each has a distinct observable statement. The CSV records
all required fields and marks 80 as not individually reviewed.

[OBSERVED] Twenty candidates were selected for a bounded adversarial screen,
prioritizing conditional, implementable claims over implausible universal
extensions. The screen covers literature, implementation pointers, alternative
names and patent disclosures. It is not an exhaustive literature/patent search.
Closest means closest *located*, not a proven nearest work. Unresolved fields
remain UNVERIFIED. A negative search is never evidence of novelty.

The seven ranking axes are significance, novelty evidence, testability,
formalizability, feasibility, baseline capability gap and reproducibility, each
0–5. Scores are ordinal researcher judgments, not calibrated probabilities.
`prior-art-review.md` records scores and dispositions after screening. The
three detailed candidates are H005, H007 and H014, chosen for their bounded
proof obligations and independently checkable oracles. High testability does
not override the requirement for a genuine baseline separation.

[OBSERVED] H005 is the primary falsification artifact, not an accepted novel
invention. H007 and H014 receive finite-model adversarial checks. Related
strong extensions H008/H010 and H013/H015 are tested under their own original
IDs; a failed stronger claim is not quietly replaced by the weaker one.

[UNVERIFIED] No independent peer review or machine-checked universal proof is
claimed. Exact finite checks and written mathematical arguments have separate
labels. Preprocessing, metadata, sensor information and dependency checks are
charged to the mechanism. Removing a scan by precomputing its answer is not a
new capability if the conventional baseline can do the same.

[OBSERVED] Hypothesis statements and the experiment protocol are frozen before
execution; their SHA-256 values are recorded in the experiment results. Status
changes live in result/review files, never in the original CSV. All failures,
mutants, counterexamples and raw outcomes are retained. Any later discovery
round must use new IDs and a new protocol rather than reinterpret this one.
